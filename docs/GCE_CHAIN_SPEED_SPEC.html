P3 · Pre-Entry Slot Awareness — the Right-Entries pillar

Subscribe to a candidate's pool at arm, before commitment. The last 2–3 slots of real flow become the final gate: a pool whose depth is already stepping down, or whose flow is one wallet selling into manufactured buys, never receives our ticket. This targets the insta-drain class that churned live's sub-floor tickets (65 cuts in one day for ±pennies).

Impact on Basket Harvesting — the golden engine at chain fidelity

The harvest was the golden window's entire profit (+$2,073.96 across 57 sweeps). Speed + visibility upgrade every stage of it:

  • Truth-counted baskets: the harvest button and auto-harvest count sellable greens from chain depth, not aggregator marks — the "clicked 19, banked 2" sellability disconnect becomes impossible.
  • Sweep into strength, verified live: the sweep sells while the flow tape shows real bids — harvesting into the buyers we can see, not the marks we hope for. Manual clicks (your +$36/sweep-exit edge, 80–92% captures today) get the same live depth strip you'd want before pulling the trigger.
  • Atomic basket exits (P2): multi-position sweeps ride priority-fee bundles — the whole basket lands in adjacent slots instead of dribbling through 5s cycles while the wave turns.
  • Float you can watch accumulate: the cards' live strips make the basket's build-up visible in real time — the operator sees the harvest moment forming, the auto-harvest fires on chain-confirmed depth when unattended.
