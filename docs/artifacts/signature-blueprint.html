Hermes Signature Blueprint
Hermes OS · Trade Matrix Wed 15 Jul – Mon 20 Jul 2026 · n=11,896

Five signatures, five genomes.

The system runs one entry rule and one exit profile against five populations that behave nothing alike. This is the measured shape of each — how it is recognised at the qualifying tick, and what its own trajectory demands of the floor, the trail and the ladder. Every observable is read at or before entry; every outcome is scored strictly after it.

Where the current build contradicts the data

Stops are tighter than the survival band of every class

HARD_STOP_PCT = 5 · LIVE_STOP_PCT = 28. Winning climbers dip to 0.37× of entry (−63%); risers to 0.73× (−27%); moons to 0.52× (−48%). A 5% stop sits below every signature's survival band. We are stopping out winners as routine, not exception.

The trail ejects winners mid-move

TRAIL_TIGHT_PCT = 5 · TRAIL_MID_PCT = 25. Median give-back before the real high is 34.6% for climbers, 24.2% for moons, 15.5% for risers. The 5% trail ejects every class at the median; the 25% mid trail is still below what a climber needs.

The ladder banks below the median outcome

TP0 1.15 (40%) · TP1 1.30 (50%) · TP2 1.58 (80%). Every signature's median run is 1.43–1.56×. We sell half the position under the typical outcome and 80% of it by 1.58×, leaving 20% to carry a moon tail that reaches 4.27× at p90.

Three selection rules point the wrong way

  • We size up on high buy share. High buy share marks risers — the low-ceiling class. Moons carry buy share under 50%.
  • We treat deep drawdown as danger. Prior dip improves both axes monotonically: 25–45% dip → 14.5% rug / 32.9% double, versus flat → 19.0% / 27.7%.
  • We treat deep liquidity as safety. $30k+ pools are the highest rug bucket (32.6%) with zero 5× outcomes.

The Trade DNA genome is inverted

docs/trade-dna-health.md states the genome as "winners run clean (median DD-before-peak 0%); deep early DD (≥40%) is the rug/give-back tell." Measured medians say the reverse: rug 0.9%, dud 5.2%, riser 9.9%, climber 29.6%, moon 60.5%. The DNA — and the classifier prior stallDrawdownPct 22 that follows from it — currently treats the moonshot signature as the rug tell.

We see only 36% of climbers and 9.5% of risers inside the current window — but the current window is the best window on every quality metric. Reaching the rest by extending the clock means buying the same names with less move left: a climber caught at 8m has six minutes of runway to its 14m peak instead of eleven. The edge is selection within the window, not more time.

Open questions before this becomes settings

  • The floor needs an expected-value pass. floor_p10 shows where winners survive, not where a stop is optimal. Setting a climber floor at 0.37× maximises winner retention but deepens every loss that never recovers. The survival band is an input to that calculation, not the answer.
  • Climber is the thinnest cell at n=61, and it carries the most extreme prescriptions (widest floor, widest trail). It should be re-measured as the sample grows before its numbers are hard-coded.
  • Routing is priority-ordered and untested as such. A candidate can satisfy several signatures at once; the order used here (rug-risk → moon → climber → riser) is a reasonable default, not a measured one.
  • Live basis mismatch is unresolved. Live mirrors paper's exit decisions but the trigger multiple is computed against paper's entry price. Live TP0 fills at a 1.019× median against paper's 1.154× with zero slippage refusals — the gap is upstream of execution, and per-signature exits cannot be trusted in the live lane until it is found.
