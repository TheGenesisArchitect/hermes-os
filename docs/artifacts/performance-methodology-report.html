The Niche & the Perpetual Capital Engine

The niche. Hermes trades one narrow, deliberately-unglamorous slice of crypto: the first minutes of newly-graduated Solana micro-cap tokens on bonding-curve and automated-market-maker venues (PumpSwap, Meteora, Fluxbeam, pump.fun). It is the highest-velocity, most convex corner of the entire market — hundreds of launches a day, most worthless within minutes, a handful running 5×–100×+. Serious capital ignores it because it is too fast, too small, and too adversarial to trade by hand. That is precisely the moat: it can only be worked by a machine that is present at the moment of demand, every time, autonomously.

The Data Flywheel & Rug-Risk Model sim

The recorder watches every safety-passed launch — traded or not — labeling each outcome. That proprietary dataset (11,058 labeled outcomes · 257,624 trajectory ticks, growing continuously) trains a transparent logistic model that prices each candidate's rug probability at entry. Position size scales down as predicted risk rises; nothing is vetoed, everything is priced.

Validation is time-ordered — train on the past, test on the unseen most-recent 30%. Held-out AUC 0.79. The cleanest risk-quintile rugs ~40× less often than the riskiest, and the model is re-fit as the dataset grows.

08

Methodology, in Brief

Discover. Multi-source ingestion surfaces every new Solana launch within seconds. Screen. On-chain safety checks reject honeypots, live mint/freeze authority, and concentrated holders; only zero-upside traps are vetoed — everything else is priced. Confirm. No blind entries: the recorder watches each candidate and arms it only on proven, sustained demand. Size. ML rug-risk score × demand quality × venue conviction. Manage. A 5-second loop runs a take-profit ladder, ratcheting profit locks, earned-leash trailing, venue-aware stops, and the portfolio basket harvest — winners banked into strength, losers cut mechanically. Learn. Every outcome feeds the flywheel that retrains the models.

Scaling & Compounding Scenarios illustrative — not a forecast

A capacity finding shapes everything: the system is arrival-limited, not capital-limited. It runs a handful of concurrent positions because confirmed opportunities arrive ~1–2/minute and recycle in ~90 seconds — adding capital adds size per trade, not more trades, until pool depth pushes back (~$15k wallet). The scenarios scale the measured engine under explicit governors, with all profit redeployed. The live pilot's execution gap is why the retention axis exists — and why the conservative path is the honest planning case.

Scale-in doctrine. Live capital enters in stages gated by realized performance, never ahead of it: the $60 pilot → banked profit redeploys (compounding funded by the market, not fresh deposits) → contributions pause at the ~$15k capacity ceiling per wallet.
