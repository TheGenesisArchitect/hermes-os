Executive summary

Risk controls shipped this week

Each control below responds to a measured failure mode, is enforced in code on every position, and writes an audit record on every activation.

Per-position exposure limit — 25 bps of book NAV, universal
No position, in any tier, may exceed 0.25% of the book (~$6.75 at current research NAV). Basis: three over-limit positions accounted for −$47 in 90 minutes on Thursday; capped equivalents would have lost ~$8. Uniform sizing is the compounding design: winners pay through frequency, not concentration.
Three-tier sizing ladder
Full unit (25 bps): assets passing the complete qualification — verified-winner holder base, liquidity-inflow envelope, entry-timing window. Half unit: qualified assets with one degraded dimension. Discovery minimum ($1.50–3, Research Book only): everything else that merits measurement. Nothing trades above its tier.
Structured profit-taking with a milestone-indexed residual floor
Positions bank fixed tranches into strength (first at +22%), and the residual rides behind a floor that ratchets up at price milestones (1.5×, 2×, 3×, 5×…). Ten-day replay across 888 qualified positions, with fills constrained to actual pool liquidity at each tick: this exit architecture produced $1,585 vs $348 booked SIMULATED — the residual tranche is where the strategy's convexity lives.
Automated liquidity-evaporation exit
The dominant loss mode is a counterparty withdrawing pool liquidity while price still quotes. Positions now liquidate immediately when pool depth drops below exit-viability — forensics showed a 60–90 second sellable window that price-based stops always missed. Three activations in production on day one converted probable full losses into exits at −$0.07 to −$0.57.
Pre-commitment exit-route verification (production)
The Production Book will not enter any position whose exit cannot be routed at entry time. During Thursday's ~40-minute market-wide quote-infrastructure outage, this control self-suspended all production entries with zero operator intervention and zero capital impact.

Glossary — platform terms in market terms

Counterparty-reputation graph ("the crowd")
A persistent database of wallet outcomes across token launches. Assets whose early holders include verified historical winners qualify; wallet reputations persist across launches and cannot be cheaply manufactured — this is the strategy's core information edge.
Liquidity-inflow envelope
Qualified assets must show pool-liquidity growth of 1.20–2.05× at entry. Below the band correlates with stagnant launches; above it with manufactured spikes preceding withdrawal.
Adversarial liquidity withdrawal ("rug")
The counterparty removes pool liquidity, typically atomically. Unhedgeable post-entry beyond the profit-taking schedule; the strategy prices it as a known loss rate bounded by the unit cap.
Discovery positions ("probes")
Minimum-size Research-Book-only positions on non-qualified flow. They exist to keep every market segment continuously measured so qualification thresholds are re-validated against live data daily — the platform's research budget, bounded to ~$1.50–3 per observation.
Two-book architecture
Research (simulated) trades everything and takes measured risk to learn; Production (live) trades only proven cells. Research reveals; production confirms.
