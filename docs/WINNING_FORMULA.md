**Rule: venue allocation is the master variable.** Farm venues are capped to a
minority of the book (FARM_MAX_SLOTS); slots held open for organic-venue
confirms are not idle capital — they are the edge.

## Law 3 — The centaur outperforms the bot

`manual_harvest: +$150.89 across 30 clicks (+$5.03/trade)` — the single most
profitable strategy in the ledger is the human noticing "the book is green, bank
it now." The system's job is to make that judgment cheap (live float, one
button) and to automate its *pattern* (basket harvest), never to remove the
human. Main Street translation: this is a power tool, not a replacement.

## Law 4 — The adversary adapts intra-night; only live probes price him

The ×0.5 scalp experiment failed its falsifier in 26 closes because the farm
pulled pre-TP1 at 50% vs the 32% its own 24h history showed. **A backtest on
yesterday's farm cannot price today's farm.** Therefore: off-hours runs
permanently as a ×0.1 probe lab — every mechanism live, tuition ~$5/window,
data through all 12 windows. Dark hours are wasted hours.

fills ≡ positions ≡ equity, proven on-screen every render. The recon strip

## The formula, one line

**Fish organic ponds (venue caps) · in the right hours (session posture) ·
enter only confirmed demand (recorder-as-scout) · bank into strength (ladder,
no runner for the farm) · keep the human's finger on the harvest · probe what
you can't predict · gate every scale-up · reconcile everything.**
