1 · Mission & the master equation

E[moon P&L] = arrivals × P(board) × P(ride) × capture × size. The platform finds the highest-probability opportunities, takes the shot at uniform size, and manages the trade flawlessly back to the wallet. Two-book architecture: the Research Book (simulated) trades everything and probes the periphery; the Production Book (live capital) trades only proven cells and follows research sizing by construction (the mirror fraction). Benchmarks binding on the desk: armed capture ≥40%/class · gross wins:losses ≥1.5 daily · rungless-death tax ≤25% of gross wins · live drag ≤5pp · boarding = 100% of qualified triggers (measured at 100%, Jul 25).
